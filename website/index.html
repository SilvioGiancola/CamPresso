  • A Mac with hardware HEVC encoding

  • Homebrew installed
  • Your personal System Photo LibraryShared Photo Libraries aren’t supported for compression.

Full installation guide ↗
  1. 1

    Download the project.

    Get the source ZIP and unzip it in Finder. Keep the whole folder together.

    Download source ZIP ↓

    Source code with a local installer, rather than a prebuilt app.

  2. 2

    Run the installer.

    Inside CamPresso-main, double-click Install.command. It installs the required tools, builds CamPresso and opens the app.

    Installed at ~/Applications/CamPresso.app. To update, quit the app and run the latest installer again.

    Prefer Terminal, or the command won’t open?

    Open Terminal, type cd (with a space), drag the extracted folder into the window, then press Return. Run:

    bash Install.command

    Local builds use an ad hoc signature and aren’t notarized. Check the source before running the installer. Permission prompts may return after updates.

  3. 3

    Connect your Photos library.

    Allow Full Photos Access. Open CamPresso → Settings…, select your .photoslibrary, confirm it’s a personal System Photo Library and accept the review notice.

    In Photos → Settings → General, check which library is the System Photo Library. CamPresso remembers your selection for next time.

  4. 4

    Try one video. Then make the call.

    Select a video and click Compress. Leave Standard / 1080p HEVC selected to begin. When it’s ready, open Review, compare playback and choose Keep Compressed or Revert.

A few useful answers

Before you press Compress.

Will CamPresso delete my original videos automatically?

Compression adds a verified smaller copy to Photos and keeps the original. Only choosing Keep Compressed moves that matching original to Recently Deleted. Revert removes the generated compressed copy instead.

Review decisions can also be applied to selected results as a confirmed batch. After keeping a result, recovery is through Photos’ Recently Deleted.

What about videos that are only in iCloud?

CamPresso asks Photos to retrieve the current version when you compress it. One video can download while another converts. Photos supplies download percentages when available; otherwise the app shows activity. If a cloud download fails, that video remains available for retry while other videos can continue.

Both copies are in Photos during review. With iCloud Photos enabled, imports and review decisions synchronize through iCloud. You need a connection and enough local space for retrieval and conversion.

When will my Mac or iCloud have more free space?

The app reports library size reduction, rather than instantly reclaimed disk space. Photos normally keeps removed originals in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Synchronization and Photos’ own cache also affect when space becomes available.

Permanent deletion is your choice in Photos. CamPresso never empties Recently Deleted. Read Apple’s deletion guide ↗

Does it work with HDR, edited or portrait videos?

Portrait orientation is retained and smaller videos aren’t upscaled. CamPresso compresses Photos’ current rendered version, including supported edits, trims and slow motion. Supported HLG/HDR10 is preserved; unsupported media is skipped with a reason.

Dolby Vision dynamic metadata, editable Cinematic effects, faces, edit history and some Photos metadata cannot transfer. Read format and metadata limitations ↗

Where does the processing happen?

Encoding runs locally on your Mac using Apple’s media frameworks. CamPresso does not use a separate video-processing server. Photos may contact iCloud to download videos and synchronize the copies and decisions.

CamPresso releases its safe temporary media when it is no longer needed, and retains unresolved recovery material. Photos manages its own downloads and cache. The app does not delete files inside your Photos library.

What if I close the app or an operation is interrupted?

Closing during work asks whether to stop downloads/conversion and close. Finished reviews persist. An active Photos transaction is allowed to finish safely.

If an import or removal is uncertain, CamPresso retains its journal and offers inspection rather than automatically repeating the write. Use Inspect Photos status for items marked Needs attention.

I’m stuck installing or connecting Photos. Where do I go?

If the library won’t load, check its saved path and grant CamPresso Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then reopen it. Full Photos Access is also required. Family Shared Photo Libraries remain browse/play-only.

Follow the user guide or report an issue on GitHub with your macOS version, app version and error message. Don’t include private videos or library data.
